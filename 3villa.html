VILLA CONCEPT

EXPERIENCE INCLUSIVE ECO-LIVING

Villa three offers a visionary concept in luxury living, seamlessly blending with its lush surroundings. Designed to emphasise the connection between living spaces and bedrooms this residence creates an inviting all-day outdoor living experience with its covered pergolas, the outdoor seating area featuring a firepit, and the incorporation of serene water elements

Courtyards run parallel to the interior spaces, seamlessly blending the indoor and outdoor environments. To the left, there is an open semi-outdoor space featuring a built-in seating area, perfect for relaxation. In front of the elevated terrace lies an infinity pool, accessible via small steps from the veranda. The indoor living area boasts expansive openings, creating a harmonious indoor-outdoor flow.

WALKING THROUGH

Walking through Villa three is an experience of unparalleled elegance and thoughtful design. You enter from a private, beautifully crafted entrance on the western edge of the property. A paved path and a small staircase guide you through the meticulously landscaped surroundings to a sheltered exterior parking area with a wooden pergola overhead.Nearby, a cosy veranda, also shaded by a wooden pergola, invites you to relax. The interior features a warmly inviting open-plan living and kitchen area, with a dedicated laundry space for added convenience. Three exquisite bedrooms, including one with an en-suite bathroom, are accessible via internal corridors, while an additional loft room awaits your personalised touch

A mezzanine offers a private sitting area, leading to an exterior terrace, enhancing the sense of luxurious space. Villa K3 also features two well-appointed guest bathrooms, ensuring comfort and convenience for all residents.

SUSTAINABLE BUILDING & LIVING

Villa three exemplifies eco-friendly elegance, harmoniously blending with the natural landscape to provide an environmentally responsible living space. Constructed with sustainable materials, this upscale residence merges luxury with ecological awareness, setting a new benchmark in premium real estate for those who appreciate both opulence and environmental stewardship
