Pogonia Palairos, Greece

LOCATION

POGONIA PALEROS, GREECE, IS A HIDDEN GEM NESTLED ALONG THE IONIAN SEA. IN THIS TRANQUIL ENVIRONMENT, YOU CAN FIND INSPIRATION AND RECONNECT WITH YOUR MIND AND SOUL.

THE AREA IS ADORNED WITH NATIVE PLANTS SUCH AS OLIVE TREES, CYPRESS, AND WILD OREGANO, WHICH CONTRIBUTE TO ITS LUSH, FRAGRANT LANDSCAPES.

Residents and Pogonia Palerou visitors enjoy activities like hiking, sailing, and exploring the pristine beaches.

Historically, Pogonia Palerou has roots dating back to ancient Greece... etc.

ACCESS

Pogonia Residential Complex is about a 4 hr drive from Athens... and 2 h from Patra, two of the biggest ports of Greece.

IOANNIAN SEA

LUSH OLIVE GROVES AND WILD MEDITERRANEAN HERBS COVER MOST OF POGONIA'S LANDSCAPES

Immerse yourself in breathtaking hiking and sailing adventures or unwind on pristine beaches. Moreover, Pogonia’s authentic local cuisine offers a true taste of tradition, while its enchanting surroundings provide an unparalleled escape for you and your family.
