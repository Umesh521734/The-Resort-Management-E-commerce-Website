THE EIGHT'S MANIFESTO

YOUR LIFELONG ECO-LIVING IN POGONIA

The Eight is a real estate project that comprises eight energy-efficient, eco-friendly villas. The residences blend effortlessly with the natural landscape, featuring a central atrium and courtyards that beautifully reflect Greek cultural heritage. The design fosters a fluid transition between indoors and outdoors, redefining those boundaries for a harmonious architectural canvas.

EMBRACING THE SUSTAINABLE LIFESTYLE

The idea for this uniquely designed luxury villa complex embraces the passion for sustainable architecture and the beauty of the Mediterranean nature. Inspired by the elements of water, stone, air, light, and shadow, a unique sanctuary comes alive to harmonise eco-friendly living and well-being.

SIGNATURE ELEMENTS

  • Lush Mediterranean landscape
  • Water-inspired architecture
  • Shaded parking slots
  • Social-centric outdoor lounge areas
  • Fluid indoor outdoor transition
