THE ECO-LIVING PHILOSOPHY OF THE EIGHT PRESENTS A SOPHISTICATED LIFESTYLE
HARMONIOUSLY INTERTWINED WITH NATURE.

The eco-living philosophy of The Eight presents a sophisticated lifestyle harmoniously intertwined with nature. Situated in Pogonia Palerou by the Ionian Sea, this exquisite villa complex exemplifies sustainable design and unmatched elegance. Here, high architectural design and sustainability merge, resulting in exquisite, eco-friendly living spaces that last.
