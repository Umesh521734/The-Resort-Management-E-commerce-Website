VILLA CONCEPT

FIND YOUR INNER PEACE WITHIN NATURE’S ARMS

Villa one is designed with an emphasis to connection between living spaces and bedrooms. The villa’s shape seamlessly aligns with the natural landscape celebrating the beauty of Mediterranean nature. The two-level residential space offers a unique view to the surroundings while blurring the boundaries between indoor and outdoor living. Abundant windows throughout the building ensure ample natural light and ventilation.

Moreover, the building’s perimeter is richly planted with trees and shrubs, while portions of the roof are green, enhancing the overall greenery. Accessibility is paramount, with unobstructed, fully functional access meticulously planned to accommodate wheelchair use and other mobility aids.

WALKING THROUGH

You can enter Villa one through a private entrance on the western edge of the property, following a paved path that winds through beautifully landscaped surroundings. The eastern section, in front of the living room and a bedroom, features an elevated terrace with a wooden pergola. The serene infinity pool lies in front of this terrace.

Adjacent to the pool, a paved path leads to an outdoor seating room. An open-plan living area and kitchen will gracefully greet you, with a guest WC and laundry area to the left. Villa K1 includes four bedrooms in total, two with en-suite bathrooms. Three bedrooms are on the main level while the spare room lies on the mezzanine and has access to the rooftop terrace. The loft room is yours to design as you wish – bedroom, playroom, guest room etc.

SUSTAINABLE BUILDING & LIVING

Villa one epitomises sustainable luxury, seamlessly integrating with the local terrain to offer an environmentally responsible living space. Crafted with sustainable materials, this high-end residence combines splendour with eco-consciousness, setting a new standard in elite real estate for those who value both luxury and respect for the environment.
